===============================================================================
                      MEETING SUMMARIZER - HUONG DAN SU DUNG
                              (Ban cho macOS)
===============================================================================

Ung dung ghi am cuoc hop, tu dong chuyen thanh van ban va viet bien ban
chi tiet bang tieng Viet.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MAY CAN GI
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - macOS 11 (Big Sur) tro len
  - Mot trinh duyet (Safari co san tren may, khong can cai them)
  - Mot khoa API Gemini mien phi (huong dan lay o muc 3)

KHONG can cai Python, khong can cai them thu vien nao. Tat ca da nam san
trong ung dung, ke ca model nhan dang giong noi.

Chon dung ban cho may cua ban:
  - Vao menu Apple () o goc tren trai > About This Mac
  - Neu thay "Apple M1 / M2 / M3 / M4"  ->  dung ban  arm64
  - Neu thay "Intel"                    ->  dung ban  x86_64
Dua sai ban thi ung dung se khong chay duoc.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAI DAT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Buoc 1. Nhay dup vao file .dmg
  Buoc 2. Keo bieu tuong "MeetingSummarizer" vao thu muc "Applications"
  Buoc 3. Dong cua so, thao (eject) o dia .dmg
  Buoc 4. Mo Applications, nhay dup "MeetingSummarizer"


  *** LAN DAU MO SE BI MACOS CHAN - DAY LA BINH THUONG ***

Ung dung chua duoc Apple chung thuc (notarize), nen macOS se hien bang
canh bao dai y "Apple could not verify this app is free of malware".
Ung dung khong co van de gi, chi la chua mua chung chi cua Apple.

Cach mo:

  * macOS 15 (Sequoia) tro len:
      1. Mo ung dung mot lan (se bi chan, bam OK)
      2. Vao  > System Settings > Privacy & Security
      3. Keo xuong cuoi trang, tim dong noi ve MeetingSummarizer
      4. Bam "Open Anyway", nhap mat khau may neu duoc hoi
      5. Mo lai ung dung

  * macOS 14 tro xuong:
      1. Nhay CHUOT PHAI vao ung dung trong Applications
      2. Chon "Open"
      3. Trong hop thoai, bam "Open" mot lan nua

Chi phai lam mot lan duy nhat. Nhung lan sau mo binh thuong.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LAY KHOA API GEMINI (MIEN PHI)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Khoa nay dung de viet bien ban cuoc hop. Chi can mot khoa duy nhat.

  1. Mo trang:  https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. Dang nhap bang tai khoan Google
  3. Bam "Create API key"
  4. Sao chep chuoi khoa (bat dau bang "AIza...")

Goi mien phi cho phep khoang 15 yeu cau moi phut va khoang 1000-1500 yeu cau
moi ngay - du cho nhieu cuoc hop trong ngay.

LUU Y VE BAO MAT: noi dung van ban cuoc hop se duoc gui len Gemini de viet
bien ban. Neu cuoc hop co thong tin bao mat, hay doc ky dieu khoan su dung
cua goi mien phi truoc khi dung. Rieng phan AM THANH thi khong bao gio roi
khoi may nay.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CAU HINH LAN DAU
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mo ung dung. No se tu mo trinh duyet.

Ban se thay:
  - Mot bang mau vang: "Chua cau hinh xong"
  - Nut banh xe rang (bieu tuong hinh banh rang) o goc tren phai, mau cam

  1. Bam vao nut banh rang
  2. Dan khoa Gemini vao o "Gemini API Key"
  3. Bam "Luu"

Bang mau vang bien mat la xong. Khoa duoc luu tren may nay, khong gui di
dau khac.

Muon doi khoa sau nay: bam lai nut banh rang, dan khoa moi, bam Luu.
Co hieu luc ngay, khong can mo lai ung dung.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GHI AM MOT CUOC HOP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. (Khong bat buoc) Nhap tieu de cuoc hop
  2. (Khong bat buoc) Nhap muc tieu / chu de chinh cua cuoc hop
     -> Neu nhap, ung dung se canh bao khi moi nguoi noi lac de tai
  3. Bam nut micro tron o giua
  4. Lan dau tien, trinh duyet se hoi quyen dung micro -> chon "Allow"
  5. Noi binh thuong. Van ban se hien dan len ngay trong luc hop
  6. Xong thi bam lai nut micro de dung

Sau khi dung, ung dung se viet bien ban chi tiet. Cho mot lat.

LUU Y: lan nhan dang dau tien sau khi mo ung dung se cham hon (khoang
10-30 giay) vi phai nap model vao bo nho. Ung dung KHONG bi treo, chi la
dang chuan bi. Tu lan thu hai tro di se nhanh.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. KET QUA LUU O DAU
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ban co the bam "Sao chep" hoac "Tai xuong .md" ngay tren trang.

File cung duoc tu dong luu vao:

      ~/Documents/MeetingSummarizer/

Trong do co hai loai file cho moi cuoc hop:
  - transcript_...md   : toan bo loi noi da nhan dang
  - summary_...md      : bien ban chi tiet

Cach mo nhanh thu muc: trong Finder bam Cmd+Shift+G roi dan duong dan tren.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THOAT UNG DUNG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dong tab trinh duyet KHONG lam ung dung tat. May chu van chay ngam.

Muon tat hoan toan:
  - Nhay chuot phai vao bieu tuong MeetingSummarizer tren Dock
  - Chon "Quit"

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bam nut banh rang de mo. Cac lua chon:

  * Model Gemini

      - Flash Lite  : nhe hon, dung khi bi bao vuot gioi han

  * Nhan dang giong noi bang

                      khong ton phi. Am thanh khong roi khoi may.
      - OpenAI      : can them khoa OpenAI rieng, co tinh phi
      - Groq        : can them khoa Groq rieng, co goi mien phi

  * Model PhoWhisper
      - Chi ban "small" duoc dong goi san trong ung dung.
      - Chon ban khac (tiny/base/medium) thi ung dung se phai TAI VE,
        can mang mot lan.

  * Thiet bi xu ly
      - Tu dong : dung GPU (Metal) neu may ho tro. Nhanh hon.
      - CPU     : cham hon nhung on dinh nhat.
        -> Neu nhan dang bi loi la, hay doi sang CPU.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. KHI GAP SU CO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Truoc khi hoi, hay lay file ghi log loi:

      ~/Library/Application Support/MeetingSummarizer/launcher.log

Cach mo: trong Finder bam Cmd+Shift+G, dan duong dan tren, bam Enter.


  VAN DE: Mo ung dung thi bi bao khong the mo / bi chan
  -> Xem lai muc 2, phan "LAN DAU MO SE BI MACOS CHAN"

  VAN DE: Bao "damaged and can't be opened"
  -> Rat co the tai sai ban (Intel / Apple Silicon). Xem muc 1.

  VAN DE: Trinh duyet khong tu mo
  -> Tu mo trinh duyet va vao dia chi:  http://127.0.0.1:8000

  VAN DE: Bam micro nhung khong hien chu nao
  -> Kiem tra da cho phep quyen micro chua:
      > System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone
     Bat cho trinh duyet dang dung (Safari / Chrome).
  -> Thu noi to hon va gan micro hon.

  VAN DE: Co van ban nhung khong ra bien ban
  -> Phan bien ban can MANG va can khoa Gemini dung.
     Kiem tra ket noi mang, va bam banh rang xem khoa con dung khong.

  VAN DE: Bao vuot gioi han (rate limit / quota)
  -> Goi mien phi Gemini co han muc. Doi vai phut roi thu lai,
     hoac vao banh rang doi sang "Flash Lite".
  -> Neu ban co nhap muc tieu cuoc hop, tinh nang canh bao lac de tai se
     goi API kha thuong xuyen. Bo trong o muc tieu se giam dang ke.

  VAN DE: Nhan dang rat cham
  -> Lan dau sau khi mo ung dung luon cham (nap model). Tu lan hai se nhanh.
  -> Neu van cham, vao banh rang doi "Model PhoWhisper" sang "base" hoac
     "tiny" (se phai tai ve mot lan).
